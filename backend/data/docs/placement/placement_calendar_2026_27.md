# Placement Calendar 2026-27

## Registration window
Registration on the placement portal is open from 1 August 2026 to 30 September 2026. A student who has not completed registration by 30 September 2026 cannot attend campus drives this season.

## Training
Aptitude training and mock interviews run from 1 September 2026 to 10 October 2026.

## Placement season
The placement season runs from 15 September 2026 to 30 April 2027. Company drives are scheduled as companies confirm their dates.

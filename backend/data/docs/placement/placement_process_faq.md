# Placement Process FAQ

## How do I prepare my resume?
A resume must be a PDF of at most two pages. Use the template on the placement portal, list projects and internships with dates, and do not include a photograph.

## Are there mock interviews?
Yes. The Placement Cell runs mock interviews and aptitude practice sessions from September to October. Registration for these sessions is on the placement portal and is free.

## What is the selection process for a drive?
Most drives have an online aptitude test, a technical round and an HR interview. The drive notice for each company lists the exact rounds.

## Do I have to attend the pre-placement talk?
Yes. Attending the pre-placement talk of a company is mandatory for every student who registered for that company's drive. The talk is held on the day before the written test.

## When do offer letters arrive?
Companies release offer letters within 4 weeks of the final round. The Placement Cell forwards each letter to the student, who must respond within 48 hours.

## Can I apply to companies outside campus placements?
Yes, but you must inform the Placement Cell in writing before you accept any off-campus offer. An off-campus offer counts as an offer under the one-offer policy.

## Can I opt out of placements to study further?
Yes. Students planning higher studies can opt out on the placement portal. An opt-out can be reversed only during the registration window.

## What if I miss the registration deadline for a drive?
Late registration is not accepted. Check the deadline shown for each drive on the placement portal.

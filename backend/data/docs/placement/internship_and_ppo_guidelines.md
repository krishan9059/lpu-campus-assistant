# Internship and PPO Guidelines

## Summer internships
A summer internship must last at least 8 weeks. Students must get a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the department coordinator before joining. The NOC is issued within 3 working days.

## Credit transfer
An approved internship of at least 8 weeks can be converted into 2 academic credits. The student submits the internship certificate and a report within 2 weeks of returning.

## Pre-placement offers
An offer received through an internship is a pre-placement offer (PPO). A PPO counts as an offer under the one-offer policy, and the student must inform the Placement Cell within 7 days of receiving it.

## Stipend
The stipend is decided by the company. The university does not fix or guarantee a stipend.

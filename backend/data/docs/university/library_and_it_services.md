# Library and IT Services

## Library timings
The central library is open from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, Monday to Saturday. During the examination period the reading room stays open 24 hours a day.

## Borrowing
An undergraduate student can borrow 4 books for 14 days. A postgraduate student can borrow 6 books for 21 days. A book can be renewed once if no one else has reserved it.

## Fines
The fine for an overdue book is Rs 2 per day per book. A lost book must be replaced or paid for at its current price plus a processing charge of Rs 100.

## Digital library
E-journals and e-books can be accessed from any device through the library portal, using the student portal credentials. Off-campus access is through the university VPN.

## Portal password
The student portal login is the registration number. A forgotten password can be reset from the login page using the registered mobile number, or at the IT Service Desk with the ID card.

## Wi-Fi
Each student can register up to 3 devices for campus Wi-Fi from the student portal. A newly registered device becomes active within 15 minutes.

## University e-mail
Every student receives a university e-mail address at admission. It is used for all official communication, so check it regularly.

# Certificates and ID Cards

## ID card
The first ID card is issued free of charge at admission. A replacement for a lost card costs Rs 300 and needs an affidavit or a copy of the FIR. Replacement cards are issued within 3 working days.

## Bonafide certificate
A bonafide certificate is requested on the student portal and is ready within 2 working days.

## Transcripts
Official transcripts are requested on the student portal. The fee is Rs 500 per sealed copy and delivery takes 7 working days.

## Provisional degree and degree certificate
A provisional degree certificate is issued within 15 days of the final result, once the student has no-dues clearance. The degree certificate is awarded at the convocation.

## No-dues clearance
No-dues clearance needs sign-off from the Library, the Hostel Office, the Accounts Section and the student's department. Start the request on the student portal at least 10 working days before the certificate is needed.

## Corrections to records
A correction of name or date of birth needs the original Class 10 certificate and a notarised affidavit, submitted to the Registrar's office. Corrections take up to 15 working days.

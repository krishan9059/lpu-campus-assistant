# Notice: Extension of the second fee instalment date

This notice supersedes the notice dated 1 September 2026. The last date to pay the second instalment of the term fee for Odd Term 2026-27 is extended to 15 October 2026. The late fee applies from 16 October 2026. The extension applies to all undergraduate and postgraduate students.

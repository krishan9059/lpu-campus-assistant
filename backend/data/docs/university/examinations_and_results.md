# Examinations and Results

## Schedule
The MST is held in weeks 8 and 9 of the term. The ETE begins after teaching ends and runs for about three weeks. The date sheet is published on the student portal at least 30 days before the first ETE paper.

## Hall ticket
The hall ticket can be downloaded from the student portal 5 days before the first ETE paper. Students must carry the hall ticket and the university ID card to every examination. A student without an ID card may sit the paper only after the invigilator verifies and records the student's identity.

## Conduct in the examination hall
Only non-programmable scientific calculators are allowed, and only where the question paper permits them. Mobile phones and smart watches must be switched off and left outside the hall. Use of unfair means results in cancellation of the paper and may lead to further disciplinary action.

## Results
Results are declared within 21 days of the last ETE paper and are published on the student portal.

## Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation of an ETE answer script within 7 days of the result declaration. The application is made on the student portal and the fee is Rs 800 per paper. The outcome is announced within 15 days. The revised marks replace the original marks, whether they are higher or lower.

## Make-up examination
A student who misses an ETE paper because of a medical emergency may apply for a make-up examination within 3 days of the missed paper. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required. The make-up examination is held within 30 days.

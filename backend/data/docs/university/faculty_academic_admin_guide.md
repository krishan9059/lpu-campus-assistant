# Faculty Guide to Academic Administration

## Course file
The course file must be submitted to the department coordinator within 2 weeks of the start of the term.

## Attendance entry
Attendance must be entered on the portal within 48 hours of each class. Later entries need the approval of the Head of Department (HoD).

## Marks entry deadlines
MST marks must be entered within 7 days of the test, and ETE marks within 10 days of the paper.

## Question papers
The ETE question paper and answer key must reach the Examination Cell at least 21 days before the examination.

## Invigilation duty
Invigilation duty is allotted 3 days before the ETE begins. Swaps need the approval of the Examination Cell.

## Timetable changes
A class can be rescheduled with the HoD's approval, requested at least 48 hours in advance. Students must be informed through the portal.

## Guest lectures
A guest lecture needs the approval of the Dean of the school at least 7 days before the event.

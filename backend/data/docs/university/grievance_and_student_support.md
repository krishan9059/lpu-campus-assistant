# Grievance and Student Support

## Grievance redressal
A grievance is handled at three levels. First, submit it to the department coordinator, who responds within 5 working days. If it is not resolved, take it to the Grievance Redressal Committee, which decides within 10 working days. A student who is still not satisfied may appeal to the Dean of Student Welfare within 15 days of the committee's decision.

## Counselling
The Student Counselling Centre offers free and confidential counselling. Appointments are booked on the student portal, or students can walk in during working hours.

## Health centre
The campus health centre is open 24 hours a day and has an ambulance on call. Basic consultation is free for enrolled students.

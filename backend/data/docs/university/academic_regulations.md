# Academic Regulations

## Academic year
The academic year has two terms: the odd term (August to December) and the even term (January to May). Each term has about 18 teaching weeks, followed by the examination period.

## Attendance
A student must attend at least 75% of the classes held in each course to be allowed to sit the term-end examination (ETE). Attendance is marked by the course instructor in every class and appears on the student portal within 48 hours.

### Condonation of shortfall
A student with attendance between 65% and 74.99% in a course may apply for condonation. The application needs a medical certificate or an approved-event letter, and a condonation fee of Rs 1,000 per course. Condonation is granted once per course. A student below 65% is detained in that course and must register for it again in a later term.

## Evaluation scheme
Every course is assessed through continuous assessment (CA) worth 30 marks, the mid-semester test (MST) worth 20 marks and the term-end examination (ETE) worth 50 marks. To pass a course a student needs at least 40 marks out of 100 in total and at least 35% of the marks in the ETE.

## Grading and CGPA
Marks are converted to grades on a 10-point scale: O (10 points) for 90 and above, A+ (9) for 80 to 89, A (8) for 70 to 79, B+ (7) for 60 to 69, B (6) for 50 to 59, C (5) for 45 to 49, D (4) for 40 to 44 and F (0) below 40.

The SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points in one term. The CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all completed terms.

## Re-appear examinations
A student who fails a course (grade F) may take the re-appear examination in the next re-appear window. The re-appear fee is Rs 1,200 per course. A student has at most two re-appear attempts per course. After that the course must be taken again in a regular term.

## Grade improvement
A student may improve the grade of up to two courses in the final year by appearing again in the ETE. The better of the two grades is kept. The grade improvement fee is Rs 1,500 per course.

# Fees and Refunds

## Payment modes
Fees are paid online through the student portal by UPI, net banking or card. A bank challan or a demand draft in favour of the university is also accepted at the Accounts Section. Cash is not accepted.

## Fee instalments
The term fee is paid in two instalments. The first is due within two weeks of the start of the term. The second is due by the date announced in the fee notice for that term. A later notice replaces an earlier one, so always check the most recent notice on the student portal.

## Late fee
A late fee of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs 5,000 per instalment. Registration for the next term is blocked while a fee instalment is overdue.

## Receipts
A fee receipt is generated on the student portal within one working day of an online payment. Payments made by challan or demand draft are updated within three working days.

## Refunds
A student who withdraws from the university receives a refund of tuition fee as follows: 90% if the request is received at least 30 days before the term begins, 50% if it is received within 15 days after the term begins, and no refund after that. The registration fee is not refundable. The security deposit is refunded within 30 days after the student obtains no-dues clearance.

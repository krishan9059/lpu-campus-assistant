# Scholarships and Financial Aid

## Merit scholarship
Merit scholarships are awarded at admission on the basis of entrance or board-exam scores and are given as a percentage waiver of tuition fee.

## Renewal
To renew a merit scholarship for the next year, a student must have a CGPA of at least 7.5 at the end of the year. A CGPA of 8.5 or above qualifies for the higher waiver tier. A student whose CGPA falls below 7.5 loses the scholarship for the following year.

## How to apply
Scholarship applications are made on the student portal during the window announced each year. The documents required are mark sheets, an income certificate (for need-based aid), a passport-size photograph and an identity proof.

## Sports and cultural quota
Students who represent the university at state level or above in sports or cultural events can apply for a waiver of up to 25% of tuition fee. The application must be recommended by the Sports Department or the Cultural Affairs office.

## Government scholarships
The Scholarship Cell helps students apply through national and state scholarship portals. The completed form must reach the cell at least 7 days before the portal's own closing date so that the university can verify it.

## Disbursement
Scholarship amounts are adjusted against the fee of the next instalment. Cash is not paid to students.

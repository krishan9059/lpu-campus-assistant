# Hostel and Campus Life

## Allotment
Hostel rooms are allotted after fee payment and confirmed on the student portal. A student may request a room change once a year through the portal. Requests are considered subject to availability.

## Gate timings
Hostel gates close at 10:00 pm for undergraduate residents. A student who will return later needs late-entry permission from the warden in advance.

## Leave-out
A student who wants to spend a night outside the hostel applies for a leave-out on the student portal at least 24 hours in advance. Students below 18 years need a parent's consent.

## Visitors
Visitors are received only in the visitor lounge, between 10:00 am and 6:00 pm. Visitors are not allowed in rooms.

## Mess
The mess serves breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner. A student who will be away for three or more consecutive days can apply for a mess rebate at the hostel office 2 days in advance.

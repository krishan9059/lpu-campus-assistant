# Campus FAQ

## What are the campus bus timings?
Shuttle buses run every 20 minutes between 7:30 am and 8:30 pm from the main gate to the academic blocks and the hostels.

## When are the administrative offices open?
Administrative offices are open from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday to Saturday. They are closed on Sundays and gazetted holidays.

## How do I get a parking sticker?
Two-wheeler and four-wheeler stickers are issued by the Security Office for Rs 200 per year. Bring the university ID card and the vehicle registration certificate.

## How do I join a student club?
Clubs recruit during the first three weeks of each term. Register under Activities on the student portal.

## Can I take a course from another department?
Yes. A student can take up to two open electives per year from other departments, with the HoD's approval on the student portal in the first week of the term.

## How do I get a No Objection Certificate (NOC) for an internship?
Request the NOC from the department coordinator on the student portal. It is issued within 3 working days.
